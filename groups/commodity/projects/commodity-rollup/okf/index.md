# Tables

* [dim_commodities](/tables/dim_commodities.md) — `Commodity` - The group catalog as every market sees it, with how many markets currently price each commodity.
* [dim_markets](/tables/dim_markets.md) — `Market` - Every market registered in the group's `markets` seed, with whether its sister is reporting and what the roll-up has received from it.
* [fct_landed_prices_daily](/tables/fct_landed_prices_daily.md) — `PriceObservation` - Every market's daily landed price side by side: as the market reports it (its currency per its unit) and in USD per the benchmark's quote unit, with the import-parity premium each market adds over the benchmark. Prices are non-additive: aggregate within one commodity and one market.
* [fct_market_spreads_daily](/tables/fct_market_spreads_daily.md) — `PriceObservation` - On days at least two markets priced a commodity: each market's landed price in USD per quote unit against the cheapest market's, as a spread and a percentage. Pure market effects — duty regimes and FX timing — because both sides stand on the same benchmark.
* [rpt_market_comparison_board](/tables/rpt_market_comparison_board.md) — `PriceObservation` - One row per commodity per market: the latest landed price on the benchmark's footing, the cheapest market to land it in, each market's spread to it, and how old each market's number is. The table behind the market comparison board.

# Metrics

* [avg_import_parity_ratio](/metrics/avg_import_parity_ratio.md) - Landed over benchmark, both in USD per quote unit — 1.15 means landing adds 15%. Group by commodity and market.
* [avg_landed_price_usd](/metrics/avg_landed_price_usd.md) - Mean landed price on the benchmark's footing. Group by commodity and market; this is the number that compares.
* [avg_spread_to_cheapest_ratio](/metrics/avg_spread_to_cheapest_ratio.md) - Mean spread as a share of the cheapest landed price, re-divided at query grain. Group by commodity and market.
* [avg_spread_to_cheapest_usd](/metrics/avg_spread_to_cheapest_usd.md) - Mean USD per quote unit a market pays over the cheapest market that day. Group by commodity and market.
* [benchmark_usd_total_where_landed](/metrics/benchmark_usd_total_where_landed.md) - Building block for avg_import_parity_ratio — the benchmark on exactly the days a landed price exists.
* [cheapest_usd_total](/metrics/cheapest_usd_total.md) - Building block for avg_spread_to_cheapest_ratio.
* [days_as_cheapest_market](/metrics/days_as_cheapest_market.md) - Days a market was the cheapest place to land a commodity. Group by commodity and market.
* [landed_usd_days](/metrics/landed_usd_days.md) - Days a market landed a commodity, restated in USD per quote unit.
* [landed_usd_total](/metrics/landed_usd_total.md) - Building block for avg_landed_price_usd. A sum of prices means nothing on its own.
* [spread_days](/metrics/spread_days.md) - Days at least two markets priced the commodity.
* [spread_usd_total](/metrics/spread_usd_total.md) - Building block for avg_spread_to_cheapest_usd.
