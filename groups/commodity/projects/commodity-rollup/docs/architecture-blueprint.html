commodity-rollup Architecture Blueprint

End-to-end architecture of commodity-rollup: data from sisters lands through dlt, is modelled by dbt in DuckDB and measured by MetricFlow, served to Wren, published by Evidence and orchestrated by Dagster. Every diagram is generated from the project's own artefacts and configuration; docs/blueprint.yaml may override any of it.

Putting these on a Miro board

  1. Copy for Miro copies the diagram as Mermaid code. In Miro, open the diagram-from-code option (Mermaid) and paste. It arrives as editable shapes and connectors.
  2. Copy image puts a high-resolution PNG on the clipboard. Paste straight onto the board. Use this where Miro's Mermaid import does not support a diagram type.
  3. Copy as table copies tab-separated rows. Paste onto the board and Miro creates a table, the same as pasting from a spreadsheet.

Principles

6 principles

PrincipleStatementEnforced by
Business logic does not cross entitiesOwn warehouse, dlt, dbt and graph; only a rollup reads sisters, read-only.CLAUDE.md, gate
dlt lands raw, dbt owns meaningNo transformation in a dlt resource; staging never joins.group CLAUDE.md
Joins come from the ontologyMDL join conditions are derived from realises edges, not guessed.pf semantic mdl
Generated files come from generatorsDocs, indexes, MDL, pages and this blueprint are regenerated, never hand-edited.pre-commit gate
Agent actions are recordedIntent, decision and execution are chained and anchored.pf provenance verify
Controls are named, not assertedEach control carries a FINOS AIR id; coverage is derived.pf air gate
