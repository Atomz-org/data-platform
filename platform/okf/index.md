# Platform ontology

Generated by `pf tool okf build --platform` from `platform/src/pf/ontology/`. Every
group's and project's bundle links its concepts here; what a role means is
declared once, for every sister.

# Concepts

* [Agreement](/concepts/Agreement.md) - A commitment between parties, with a lifecycle.
* [Contract](/concepts/Contract.md) - A negotiated agreement, usually with custom terms.
* [Currency](/concepts/Currency.md) - An ISO 4217 currency. Qualifies every money amount.
* [Customer](/concepts/Customer.md) - A party that buys. May be a person or an organization.
* [Employee](/concepts/Employee.md) - Internal actor. Rarely modelled in marts; used for attribution.
* [Event](/concepts/Event.md) - Something that happened at a point in time. Immutable.
* [Interaction](/concepts/Interaction.md) - A touchpoint (email, call, session) with a party.
* [Location](/concepts/Location.md) - A geography. Country, region, or address.
* [Order](/concepts/Order.md) - A one-off purchase commitment.
* [Organization](/concepts/Organization.md) - A legal entity. Customers may belong to one.
* [Party](/concepts/Party.md) - Any actor that can enter into an agreement.
* [Payment](/concepts/Payment.md) - Movement of money settling an agreement.
* [Product](/concepts/Product.md) - A sellable thing. Plans and SKUs are Products.
* [Refund](/concepts/Refund.md) - Negative movement of money against a prior payment.
* [Subscription](/concepts/Subscription.md) - A recurring agreement with a plan, term and status.
* [Usage](/concepts/Usage.md) - Metered consumption attributable to an agreement.

# Roles

* [currency_code](/roles/currency_code.md) - ISO 4217 code qualifying a money_amount.
* [event_time](/roles/event_time.md) - When the event occurred. Candidate agg time dimension.
* [foreign_key](/roles/foreign_key.md) - Reference to another concept instance.
* [free_text](/roles/free_text.md) - Unstructured text. Never a dimension.
* [geo_country](/roles/geo_country.md) - ISO 3166 country code.
* [money_amount](/roles/money_amount.md) - Monetary value. Requires a sibling currency_code.
* [natural_key](/roles/natural_key.md) - Business key from the source system.
* [pii_address](/roles/pii_address.md) - Postal address.
* [pii_email](/roles/pii_email.md) - Email address.
* [pii_name](/roles/pii_name.md) - Person name.
* [pii_phone](/roles/pii_phone.md) - Phone number.
* [quantity](/roles/quantity.md) - Countable measure.
* [status_enum](/roles/status_enum.md) - Lifecycle state. Monitored for category drift.
* [surrogate_key](/roles/surrogate_key.md) - Generated key
* [valid_from](/roles/valid_from.md) - SCD2 interval start.
* [valid_to](/roles/valid_to.md) - SCD2 interval end.
