acme-us Architecture Blueprint

End-to-end architecture of acme-us: data from stripe lands through dlt, is modelled by dbt in DuckDB and measured by MetricFlow, served to Wren, published by Evidence and orchestrated by Dagster. Every diagram is generated from the project's own artefacts and configuration; docs/blueprint.yaml may override any of it.

Putting these on a Miro board

  1. Copy for Miro copies the diagram as Mermaid code. In Miro, open the diagram-from-code option (Mermaid) and paste. It arrives as editable shapes and connectors.
  2. Copy image puts a high-resolution PNG on the clipboard. Paste straight onto the board. Use this where Miro's Mermaid import does not support a diagram type.
  3. Copy as table copies tab-separated rows. Paste onto the board and Miro creates a table, the same as pasting from a spreadsheet.
